Dutch Tax Treaty Policy Should Wait For Int'l Deal, NFTC Says - Law360

The National Foreign Trade Council (NFTC) has advised the Netherlands to defer new tax treaty policies until broader international consensus, particularly from OECD-led initiatives, is established. Mondaq Notable
JONES DAY PRESENTS®: Resolving Double Taxation Through MAP And APAs (Video) - Mondaq

This practitioner analysis elaborates on the critical role of Mutual Agreement Procedures (MAPs) and Advance Pricing Agreements (APAs) in resolving international double taxation. MAPs, enshrined in bilateral tax treaties, allow competent authorities to negotiate and resolve disputes concerning treaty application, often on transfer pricing adjustments. APAs provide upfront certainty by agreeing on transfer pricing methodologies for future transactions, significantly reducing litigation risk for multinational enterprises.
